Kaayar Ishq Ma

Written by Subham Mahesh (2026) · Sung by Anonymous

Mere dil ma ek kaala paani hai Na kinaara, na koi kashti hai Tera naam le ke utra tha Ab darr hai, doob na jaun

Baat zubaan tak aayi thi Hothon pe aa ke ruk gayi Tu hansti rahi, main chup raha Aur shaam yunhi dhal gayi

Main kaayar hoon tere ishq ma Kehta nahi, par bhoolta bhi nahi Main kaayar hoon tere ishq ma Kehta nahi, par bhoolta bhi nahi

Sapnon ma ek ghar sajaya tha Khidki pe teri pasand ke parde Ab tera izhaar kisi aur ka hai Mere paas bas teri aankhein, tere lafz

Apne dil ki vakaalat bhi main karun Aur qaazi bhi khud ban jaun Faisla har baar wahi sunaun Ki mera pyaar hi jhootha tha

Main kaayar hoon tere ishq ma Kehta nahi, par bhoolta bhi nahi Main kaayar hoon tere ishq ma Kehta nahi, par bhoolta bhi nahi

Likh de meri haazri apne ghar ke khaate ma Dihadi pe aa jaunga, roz, bina naaga Mazdoor hi sahi, teri khushi ka purza hi sahi Bas darwaza khula rakhna

Khuda ne tujhe mera rab banaya Ab dua bhi tujhse, shikwa bhi tujhse Ae mere rab, bas ek baar Tu bhi mujhse ishq kar

Ishq ka bukhaar hai Razai odhe pada hoon Tu poochhegi nahi, pata hai Phir bhi darwaze ki taraf dekhta hoon
